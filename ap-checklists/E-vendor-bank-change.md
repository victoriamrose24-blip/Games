# Card E — Vendor Bank / Remit-To Change

**Style: READ-DO. Triggered, never scheduled.**

**Trigger:** any new vendor, or any change to remit-to address, bank account, or
routing number — however it arrives, and however routine it looks.

This is the highest-consequence, lowest-frequency thing you touch. Business email
compromise enters accounts payable almost exclusively at this step, and it works
precisely because the request arrives when you're busy and looks completely normal.
Urgency in the request is a warning sign, not a reason to hurry.

---

- [ ] **Hold all payments to this vendor** until this card is complete.
- [ ] **Verify by phone**, using a number **already on file** in the vendor master
      or on a prior invoice — **never** a number from the email, the attachment, or
      the signature block requesting the change.
- [ ] Speak to a **known contact**, or to someone whose identity you can confirm
      independently.
- [ ] Confirm the **specific new details** back to them — read the digits aloud.
      Don't ask "did you send a change?", ask them to state the account.
- [ ] **Second person confirms** the change before it's entered `[WHO]`.
- [ ] Document it:

```
Date of request:      ____________________
Requested via:        ____________________
Verified by phone:    ____________________  (number used, and where it came from)
Spoke with:           ____________________
Date/time of call:    ____________________
Second reviewer:      ____________________
```

- [ ] Enter the change in `[YOUR SYSTEM]`.
- [ ] **Hold the vendor's next payment one cycle** `[OR YOUR POLICY]`, then release.
- [ ] File the documentation `[WHERE]`.

---

## Escalate immediately if

- The number on file doesn't work, or goes somewhere unexpected
- The contact is new, or unknown to you
- The request is urgent, or pushes back on verification
- The email domain differs from the vendor's usual domain, even by one character
- The change arrives right before a large scheduled payment

→ Escalate to `[WHO]`. Do not process. It is always acceptable to be slow here.

---

**Never-Skip items on this card:** all of them.
