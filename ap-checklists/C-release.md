## THE RE-TRANSMIT RULE

**If you were interrupted and you are not certain the file went out, do not re-send.**

Go to the bank portal and look at today's activity. The bank's record is the only
source of truth. Your memory of the last ten minutes is not, and re-sending a run
duplicates every payment in it.
